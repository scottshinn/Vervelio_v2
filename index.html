Caelion

caelion.app →

A CRM that works the room, not just stores it.

Most CRMs are filing cabinets: you put the work in, and they hand it back when you ask. Caelion is built the other way round. It reads what is happening across your tools, decides what each relationship needs next, and does it — drafting the follow-up, updating the record, nudging the deal that has gone quiet — then tells you what it did. It runs quietly underneath everything else you use, so there is no new app to live in.

  • Acts on your relationships instead of waiting for you to log them
  • Drafts follow-ups and next steps, and flags the deals going cold
  • Keeps every record tidy and current on its own
  • Runs headless behind your existing tools — no new inbox to check

Agentic CRM · Relationship automation · Headless · In development

[STAGE_03] · BUILD

Dev Djinn

Your work, woven into one map.

Your real work is scattered and mostly invisible — half-finished branches on your laptop, decisions buried in a chat that already ended, notes in five places, and nothing tying a broken deploy back to the change that caused it. Dev Djinn watches quietly in the background and weaves all of it into one map you can search, share, and pick back up tomorrow.

  • Shows the work actually in progress, not just what has been finished and filed
  • Keeps a readable record of what an AI assistant did and why, long after the chat is gone
  • Notes, tasks and code all linked together and searchable
  • Tells you what has gone quiet, what is overdue, and what just broke
  • Runs on your own machine — your code never has to leave it

Developer experience · Context handoff · Local-first

Vervelio Labs

verveliolabs.com →

We break our own agents first, so nobody else gets to.

Everything above talks to real data and takes real actions, which makes it a target. Vervelio Labs is the in-house red team that attacks each product before it ships — slipping hidden instructions into documents, tricking agents into misusing their tools, trying to walk data out the door — and then helps close every hole it finds.   • Two-week red-team sprints against a single AI app or agent
  • Findings ranked by severity, with the steps to reproduce each one

  • Every Vervelio product is tested by the crew before launch
