Products

Seven products, all built in-house. Each one has its own site with the full story.

Stage 1 · Identify

NidaMind

nidamind.com →

Know what to build before you build it.

Most product ideas start as a hunch. NidaMind starts with evidence. It reads what people complain about in public, finds the complaints that keep coming back, and tells you which ones are worth building, who has the problem and who is already trying to fix it.

  • Ideas come from real complaints, not guesses
  • Each idea comes with its likely buyer, its competitors and a first build plan
  • Spots the same complaint however people word it
  • Updates around the clock
Stage 2 · Architect

CaelionCRM

caelion.app →

A CRM that works the room, not just stores it.

Most CRMs wait for you to type things in. CaelionCRM watches your email, calendar and the tools you already use, then does the next step itself: it sends the follow-up, updates the record and flags the deal that has gone quiet. You get a note of what it did.

  • Logs your relationships for you
  • Drafts follow-ups and flags deals going cold
  • Works inside your existing tools, no new app to open
Stage 3 · Build

Dev Djinn

Your work, woven into one map.

Half-finished branches, decisions lost in old chats, notes in five places, and nothing linking a broken deploy to the change behind it. Dev Djinn runs in the background and pulls all of it into one map you can search, share and pick up tomorrow.

  • Shows work in progress, not just finished tickets
  • Keeps a record of what an AI assistant changed and why
  • Tells you what has stalled, what is late and what just broke
  • Runs on your machine, so your code stays there
Stage 4 · Launch

thewoofback.com

thewoofback.com →

An entire growth department, in one system.

Give it a topic and it researches, writes and publishes the piece. Then it tracks where you rank, which AI assistants quote you, who visited and who got in touch. It runs one brand or fifty, finds new clients, and with Fieldwork built in it also makes UGC video ads from a brief.

  • Publishes finished articles, every figure linked to its source
  • Tracks Google rankings and AI assistant mentions
  • Turns readers into leads and sends them to your inbox or CRM
  • Fieldwork: UGC video ads from script to scheduled post, checked before anything is paid for
  • Monthly client reports, written for you
Stage 5 · Scale

DoorYard

dooryardapp.com →

The most under-used asset in home services is a route that is already moving.

A crew already parked on your street can mow one more lawn for almost nothing extra. DoorYard offers that spare slot, at a set price, to the neighbours who asked to hear about it, and they book it with one tap.

  • Offers only go out when a crew is close by
  • You pay for booked jobs, not leads
  • Two extra jobs a day is around $1,600 a month
  • One tap to book, no app to install
Stage 5 · Scale

mobouros

mobouros.com →

Cold email that lands in the inbox.

Connect as many sending inboxes as you need. mobouros warms them up, spreads the sending across them and watches your reputation, so mail lands in the inbox, not spam. It finds the right people, writes a first line for each, sends the follow-ups and stops the moment someone replies.

  • Unlimited inboxes, with warm-up and rotation
  • Multi-step sequences with A/B tests
  • Built-in lead database, checked before sending
  • Every reply in one inbox, sorted by intent
  • An AI agent that answers replies and books meetings
Every stage · Security

Vervelio Labs

verveliolabs.com →

We break our own agents first, so nobody else gets to.

Everything above touches real data and takes real actions, which makes it a target. Vervelio Labs is our in-house red team. It hides instructions in documents, tricks agents into misusing their tools and tries to get data out, then helps close each hole. Now it does the same for other teams building with AI.

  • Two-week red-team sprints on one AI app or agent
  • Findings ranked by severity, with steps to reproduce
  • A retest after fixes, and a signed letter for your customers
  • Every Vervelio product is tested before launch
